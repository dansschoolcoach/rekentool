---
name: Lesson revenue allocation
description: Product decision for assigning subscription revenue to lessons and forecasting future lesson profitability.
---

Allocate the actual total monthly contribution revenue proportionally across occupied lesson places. Do not estimate each lesson as its attendance multiplied by a full average subscription fee.

**Why:** The same member can attend several lessons while receiving a package discount. Multiplying every attendance count by a full tariff duplicates revenue and makes lessons and season forecasts look too profitable. The proportional allocation always reconciles to the school's actual contribution total.

**How to apply:** Historical months use their own saved contribution and attendance. Future months carry forward the latest known contribution and attendance while recalculating scheduled lesson counts from the calendar. If active-lesson attendance is incomplete, show the allocation as unknown rather than distributing the full revenue across only the known lessons.
