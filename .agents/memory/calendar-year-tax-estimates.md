---
name: Calendar-year tax estimates
description: Durable rules for estimating tax when financial seasons cross calendar years.
---

Tax estimates are grouped by participant and January–December calendar year, independent of dance-season boundaries. Preliminary income-tax payments are annual state and must not reuse monthly tax-arrears cash outflows.

**Why:** A September–July season splits one tax year across two seasons. Season-only accumulation can materially understate the reserve, while reusing tax arrears would subtract an annual payment repeatedly from monthly cash flow.

**How to apply:** Deduplicate saved financial months by calendar month across all of the participant's seasons. Report missing months rather than silently annualizing incomplete data, reject overlaps and mixed-country years explicitly, and select the historical tax rule year from the calendar month.
