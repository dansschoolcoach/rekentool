---
name: Financial VAT input
description: Defines the user-facing VAT convention for financial amount entry.
---

Revenue, subscriptions, punch cards, venue rent, travel expenses, other activities, and ordinary costs are entered including VAT. Teacher hourly rates are including VAT only when the teacher charges VAT. Salary is entered as a gross salary, and tax payments or arrears are not VAT amounts.

**Why:** Dance schools generally know consumer prices and invoice totals including VAT, while many recover little or no input VAT. A consistent gross-entry convention is easier and reduces manual calculations, but salary and tax fields must not be mislabeled as VAT-bearing.

**How to apply:** Mark relevant web fields, Excel headers, and import previews with “incl. btw”. Preserve explicit exceptions for gross salary and tax payments. Keep older Excel headers import-compatible when wording changes.
